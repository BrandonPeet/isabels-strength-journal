---
name: Historical workout identity
description: Why chronological sequence changes must not relabel saved exercise sessions.
---
Keep saved workout identities fixed when earlier journal entries change; calculate the next session from active completed history.

**Why:** Reassigning old workout numbers could make recorded weights and repetitions appear to belong to different exercises. Preserving real training records takes priority over retroactively making the sequence look perfect.

**How to apply:** Changes to completion, recovery classification, or historical entries must update reports and the next session without rewriting previously logged exercise identities.
