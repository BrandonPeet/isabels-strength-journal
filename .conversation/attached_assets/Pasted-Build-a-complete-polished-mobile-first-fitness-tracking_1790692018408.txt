Build a complete, polished, mobile-first fitness tracking Progressive Web App for my wife Isabel.

This is meant to function like her own private fitness app/journal.

The app should be extremely simple to use, attractive, feminine, encouraging, and designed primarily for iPhone.

The app must track one full year:

September 29, 2026
through
September 29, 2027

The app must SAVE all of Isabel’s information automatically and reliably.

==================================================
CORE PURPOSE
==================================================

This app is Isabel’s one-year strength, mobility, cardio, consistency, and workout-progress journal.

Every day she should be able to:

1. Open the app.
2. Immediately see the ACTUAL current date.
3. Complete or skip her morning routine checklist.
4. State whether she trained that day.
5. If she trained, automatically receive the correct next workout in her workout sequence.
6. Log every exercise.
7. Log sets, repetitions/time, and weight.
8. See what she used the previous time she performed that exercise.
9. Take recovery days without breaking the workout sequence.
10. Review her progress at the end of each week.
11. Go back and review/edit previous days.
12. Close the app and come back later without losing anything.

The app should feel simple enough that Isabel can use it without having to understand complicated workout terminology or app settings.
